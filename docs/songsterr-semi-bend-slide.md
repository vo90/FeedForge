# Semi-harmonics with terminal slides

A qualified semi-harmonic keeps its original `semi` / `mixed` pitch policy and
pinch-style display while its finger bend and final slide-out retain their
independent source intervals. The slide synthesizer's shortened sample is not
the written bend release duration.

The rule requires a supported target at the initial attack; continuous ties on
the same fret, string and voice; repeated or omitted target markings; and a
final upward or downward direction-only slide. Finger-bend controllers must
not overlap. Existing separately timed note vibrato is preserved.

Changed harmonic nodes/kinds, delayed contact, displaced attacks, beat vibrato,
bar controls, targeted/incoming slides and other unqualified expressions keep
the existing fallback and findings. No song identity or revision is consulted.

Independent rational source reconstruction verifies the complete composition.
Packages using it require preservation contract 83 and bend evidence 22, with
`continuedSemiHarmonic` recording the initial mixed target and continuation rule.
An older contract cannot certify this combination. No FeedBack changes are needed.
