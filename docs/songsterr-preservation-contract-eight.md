# Preservation contract 8

Contract 8 identifies the corrected static tempo coordinates, fractional and
tied strum offsets, repeat-local grace borrowing, ties through unwritten time,
fermata note units, source-defined terminal timing intervals, and completed
package retention of tempo visibility. It also diagnoses out-of-range frets
and simultaneous same-string voices before claiming a playable package.

Recipe, evidence, independent verification, compatibility assessment and
publication/reuse gates advance together. Historical contracts 1–7 remain
readable; their passes cannot satisfy a contract-8 import or resolve its worklist.
The FeedPak format and existing game scoring policy are unchanged.
